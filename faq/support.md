Email support from the person who builds the product, included with every active licence and not sold separately. Hours are 09:00 to 18:00 India time on weekdays, and requests are triaged into three severity tiers with best-effort acknowledgment targets of one, two and five business days. There is no 24/7 desk, no phone line and no contractual SLA unless a signed order form commits one.

## How it works

- **One channel:** support@reamerlabs.com, for licensing and technical questions. There is no ticket portal, forum or public issue tracker.
- **One person:** requests reach the author of the software directly. The tiers below set targets; they are not a support organisation.
- **Covered hours:** 09:00 to 18:00 IST (UTC+5:30), Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in Pune. A message sent outside those hours, including straight after a market-hours incident, is picked up at the next covered hour.
- **Included:** support comes with an active licence, trial or annual, and is not metered per request.

## Severity tiers

- **Sev 1, down:** a licensed live instance is down or rejecting all orders. Acknowledged within 1 business day, and handled ahead of everything else.
- **Sev 2, degraded:** something is broken but a workaround exists. 2 business days.
- **Sev 3, question:** integration, documentation or a feature request. 5 business days.

Acknowledgment means triage has started, not that the issue is resolved. These targets are best effort; a signed order form is where the same figures become a contractual commitment. See [Can my firm pay by invoice or sign an order form?](/faq/invoice-and-order-form.html)

To escalate, reply on the same thread marked urgent and state the trading impact. For a Reamer Server incident, attach the file from `bin/reamer-diag-bundle`, which captures health, metrics and recent events with the monitoring token redacted.

## What it does not cover

- **Reference code.** The sample code in the kits can be copied and adapted, but it carries no support commitment.
- **Platforms outside the supported list.** See [Which operating systems are supported?](/faq/supported-platforms.html)
- **Expired licences.** Support ends with the term.
- **Running your system.** Both products run on your own machines, so there is no uptime commitment.

Updates released during your term are sent on request by email. For what one maintainer means for continuity, see [Reamer Labs is one person. What happens if it stops?](/faq/single-maintainer-risk.html)
