No. There is no free trial or free evaluation licence. There is a paid 30-day trial, USD 225 for Reamer Research and USD 900 for Reamer Server, which gives you the full product on the same terms as an annual licence. The 30 days start when you first activate, not when you buy.

## What the trial gives you

- **The full product.** The same kit, the same binaries and the same documents as an annual licence, with nothing switched off.
- **The same licence terms.** One seat on one machine, unlimited runs, and email support. A Reamer Research trial is licensed to one person and covers live or paper trading as well as backtesting.
- **30 days from first activation.** You can buy the key, read the kit and activate when your machine and data are ready. Deactivating and reactivating does not restart the clock.

## What to test in 30 days

- **The integration,** with your own data and your own strategy code, using the kit documents and reference code.
- **The benchmarks** on your own hardware, with the benchmark program in each kit. See [Have the benchmarks been independently verified?](/faq/are-benchmarks-verified.html)
- **The behaviour you depend on,** such as how fills, costs and order types are modelled, against what your strategy assumes.

## What to know before you buy one

- **It is not credited** toward a later annual licence. The year is a separate purchase at the full price.
- **It is card only,** through Paddle. Invoices are for annual licences.
- **It is not refundable.** All sales are final, and the trial is where the terms place the decision about fit. See the [terms](/terms.html).

## What you can check for free

Before paying anything, you can read the [Spec Notes](/notes.html), which publish how execution is modelled, and this FAQ. You can also read the benchmark method and raw data, and watch the integration recordings. See [How long does integration take?](/faq/integration-time.html)

To start a trial, see [pricing](/pricing.html).
