No. There is no free trial or free evaluation licence. Reamer Research needs no trial: it is USD 20 a month and you can cancel any time, so the first month does that job. Reamer Server has a paid 30-day trial at USD 2,000, which gives you the full product on the same terms as an annual licence. The 30 days start when you first activate, not when you buy.

## What a first month or a trial gives you

- **The full product.** The same kit, the same binaries and the same documents as any other licence, with nothing switched off.
- **The same licence terms.** One seat on one machine, unlimited runs, and email support. A Reamer Research seat is licensed to one person and covers live or paper trading as well as backtesting.
- **Time from first activation.** The term counts from the key's first activation, so you can buy the key, read the kit and activate when your machine and data are ready. Deactivating and reactivating does not restart the clock.

## What to test

- **The integration,** with your own data and your own strategy code, using the kit documents and reference code.
- **The benchmarks** on your own hardware, with the benchmark program in each kit. See [Have the benchmarks been independently verified?](/faq/are-benchmarks-verified.html)
- **The behaviour you depend on,** such as how fills, costs and order types are modelled, against what your strategy assumes.

## What to know before you buy

- **Reamer Research is month to month.** Cancel in the Paddle receipt or subscription email and no further month is charged; the key runs to the end of the month already paid.
- **The Reamer Server trial is not credited** toward a later annual licence. The year is a separate purchase at the full price. It is card only, through Paddle; invoices are for annual licences.
- **Refunds follow Paddle's policy.** Paddle, the merchant of record, reviews refund requests made within 14 days of purchase; the first month or the trial is where the terms place the decision about fit. See [Can I get a refund?](/faq/refunds.html)

## What you can check for free

Before paying anything, you can read the [Spec Notes](/notes.html), which publish how execution is modelled, and this FAQ. You can also read the benchmark method and raw data, and watch the integration recordings. See [How long does integration take?](/faq/integration-time.html)
