Your installed copy keeps running until its licence term ends, because the licence is checked on your machine, not against a server. What stops is updates, support, renewals and moving the licence to a new machine. If you need protection beyond that, you can set up a source escrow at your own cost, which releases the source to you only if Reamer Labs becomes insolvent or ends the product.

## The risk, said plainly

Reamer Labs is one person, Chaitanya Palghadmal. There is no team to take over if he stops, and no escrow is in place by default. If that alone rules it out for your firm, it is better to know now. See [Who makes Reamer Research and Reamer Server?](/faq/who-makes-it.html)

## What keeps working, and what does not

- **Keeps working:** every activated machine, until the end of the term you paid for. The licence is signed when you activate and checked locally after that, so the licence server going away does not stop it. A running process is never stopped by the licence, even at expiry.
- **Stops:** bug fixes and new releases, support, renewing when the term ends, and activating on a new machine. Licences are for 30 days or one year, so the longest you are covered without Reamer Labs is the rest of your current term.

## The source escrow

- **You set it up.** You choose and pay the escrow agent, and also pay Reamer Labs an annual participation fee for the source deposits and build checks. It is negotiated per customer, not a standard add-on.
- **No access while Reamer Labs runs.** The source stays with the agent. You cannot read it or ask for it.
- **Release only on two events:** Reamer Labs entering insolvency or being dissolved, or ending the product, either by written notice or by stopping updates and support for an agreed period. A sale of the business is not a trigger if the buyer keeps supporting the product, and neither is slow support.
- **Release is checked.** The agent needs documentary proof, Reamer Labs can object, and disputes go to arbitration, with the source staying locked meanwhile.
- **What you get:** the right to maintain your own deployment for internal use. No resale, redistribution or ownership.

The full terms are in `LICENSING.md` in each kit and summarised on the [About](/about.html) page. To discuss an escrow, write to hello@reamerlabs.com.
