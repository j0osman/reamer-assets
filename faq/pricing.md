Reamer Research is USD 20 per seat per month, billed monthly, and you can cancel any time. Reamer Server is USD 24,000 per seat per year, paid once with no automatic renewal, with a 30-day trial at USD 2,000. A seat is one product on one machine.

## The prices

- **Reamer Research:** $20 per seat per month, billed monthly. Cancel any time and the key runs to the end of the month already paid.
- **Reamer Server:** $24,000 per seat for a year, or $2,000 for a 30-day trial.

The Reamer Server year works out at $2,000 a month, and the trial costs the same as one month of it. Reamer Research has no trial: the first month is the trial, and it costs $20. There are no perpetual licences or volume discounts, and a firm pays the same price as an individual.

## What one payment covers

- **One product on one machine** for the term. Running both products on one machine needs one seat of each.
- **Unlimited runs** on that machine. You are not charged per backtest, per strategy or per order.
- **Updates released during the term,** at no extra cost, and support by email while the licence is active. Reamer Research subscribers download the latest release from the link in their licence email; Reamer Server updates are sent on request by email.
- **One person** for a Reamer Research seat. Each person on a team needs their own.

## How you pay

- **Card,** through Paddle, which acts as merchant of record and issues the invoice. Any sales tax or VAT for your country is shown at checkout. Reamer Research subscriptions and Reamer Server trials are card only.
- **Bank transfer,** for an annual Reamer Server licence, through an invoice from Paddle payable within 14 days.

The licence key and kit arrive by email once payment clears. There is no sales call and no quote to request.

## Renewal

- **Reamer Research** renews each month on the same key until you cancel. Each payment extends the key by the month paid, and the engine picks up the new end date by itself from release 4.8.1.
- **Reamer Server** never renews or charges automatically. Renewing is a new purchase, made when you choose, and ships the latest release.

For both, a process that is already running keeps running after expiry; only starting a new one needs a current licence.

## What it does not include

- **Refunds.** Handled by Paddle, the merchant of record, under its refund policy. See [Can I get a refund?](/faq/refunds.html)
