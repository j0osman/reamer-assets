Reamer Research is USD 1,800 per seat per year, and Reamer Server is USD 7,200 per seat per year. Each is paid once for the year and nothing renews automatically. A 30-day trial costs USD 225 for Reamer Research and USD 900 for Reamer Server. A seat is one product on one machine.

## The prices

- **Reamer Research:** $1,800 per seat for a year, or $225 for a 30-day trial.
- **Reamer Server:** $7,200 per seat for a year, or $900 for a 30-day trial.

Both work out at the same monthly rate, $150 and $600, and the trial costs one and a half months of that. Licences come only as a 30-day trial or a full year. There are no monthly plans, perpetual licences or volume discounts, and a firm pays the same price as an individual.

## What one payment covers

- **One product on one machine** for the term. Running both products on one machine needs one seat of each.
- **Unlimited runs** on that machine. You are not charged per backtest, per strategy or per order.
- **Updates released during the term,** sent at no extra cost when you ask by email, and support by email while the licence is active.
- **One person** for a Reamer Research seat. Each person on a team needs their own.

## How you pay

- **Card,** through Paddle, which acts as merchant of record and issues the invoice. Any sales tax or VAT for your country is shown at checkout.
- **Bank transfer,** for annual licences, through an invoice from Paddle payable within 14 days. Trials are card only.

The licence key and kit arrive by email once payment clears. There is no sales call and no quote to request.

## What it does not include

- **Renewal.** At the end of the term nothing is charged. Renewing is a new purchase, made when you choose. A process that is already running keeps running after expiry; only starting a new one needs a current licence.
- **Refunds.** All sales are final, which is what the trial is for. See the [terms](/terms.html).

To buy or start a trial, see [pricing](/pricing.html). Firms that need an order form should see [For firms](/firms.html).
